DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT — ContractGuard (3 Minutes)
=============================================


[0:00 – 0:20] OPENING
----------------------
Show: contractguard.site landing page

Narration:
"In Indonesia, price markup in project contracts is a widespread problem.
Contractors inflate material costs, vendors overcharge on services, and clients
have no way to verify whether the numbers are fair. Billions of rupiah are lost
every year — not through theft, but through contracts that no one truly audits.
We built ContractGuard — an AI-powered smart contract platform on Solana that
detects overpriced clauses, verifies milestone evidence, and locks payments in
escrow until the work is actually done."


[0:20 – 0:55] FEATURE 1 — AI CONTRACT AUDIT
---------------------------------------------
Show: Click Audit menu, then click "Try Demo Contract" button

Narration:
"First, upload your contract PDF. Just one click with our demo contract."

Show: Wait for QVAC analysis results to appear

Narration:
"Within seconds, our AI deeply analyzes the contract — providing a Fairness Score,
detecting risky clauses, and comparing prices against real-time market data.
No expensive lawyers needed to know whether your contract is fair."


[0:55 – 1:35] FEATURE 2 — CREATE ON-CHAIN CONTRACT
----------------------------------------------------
Show: Click Create, fill in form: title, total value, add 2 checkpoints

Narration:
"Once you're satisfied with the contract, deploy it directly to the Solana blockchain.
Fill in the project details, set checkpoints, and define the payment percentage
for each milestone."

Show: Click Deploy, confirm in Phantom wallet

Narration:
"Confirm through your Phantom wallet. Funds are locked in a smart escrow —
no one can touch them until proof of work is approved. This isn't a promise,
it's immutable code."


[1:35 – 2:25] FEATURE 3 — SUBMIT EVIDENCE & AI REVIEW
-------------------------------------------------------
Show: Go to Dashboard, open a contract, click Submit Evidence on the first checkpoint

Narration:
"Once the contractor completes the work, they upload proof — a PDF report or photos."

Show: Upload file, then click Review with AI

Narration:
"QVAC, our local AI engine, reads the evidence and compares it directly against
the contract terms. Are the specifications met? Are there any gaps? The AI delivers
a verdict: APPROVED or NEEDS REVISION — complete with a compliance score and
detailed findings."

Show: Review result — APPROVED badge, compliance score

Narration:
"If approved, the client can immediately release the USDt payment from escrow.
Everything is recorded on the blockchain — no manipulation possible."


[2:25 – 2:50] TECH STACK
--------------------------
Show: Landing page bottom section / sponsors

Narration:
"ContractGuard is built on Solana for speed and low fees, QVAC as our local
private and deterministic AI engine, Supabase for data storage, and Tether USDt
as the stable escrow currency."


[2:50 – 3:00] CLOSING
-----------------------
Show: Return to landing page

Narration:
"ContractGuard — because trust shouldn't rely on humans alone. Thank you."


=============================================
Total duration : ~3 minutes
Recording tips : Enable Phantom on Devnet, have a demo contract already deployed,
                 and make sure QVAC is running before you start recording.
